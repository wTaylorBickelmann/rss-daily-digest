Speaker diarization—the process of partitioning an audio stream into homogeneous segments according to individual speaker identity—is a critical component of modern speech processing pipelines. NVIDIA has introduced Nemotron 3 Diarization on the Hugging Face platform to enable developers to build real-time, multi-speaker audio applications.

Accurately determining who spoke when in a continuous audio stream is essential for downstream tasks such as automated meeting summarization, live customer support analysis, and multi-party conversational interfaces. By optimizing the model for real-time performance, Nemotron 3 Diarization helps reduce latency in applications that require immediate speaker tracking alongside automatic speech recognition.
