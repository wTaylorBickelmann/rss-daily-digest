Recent machine learning research focuses heavily on improving the steerability, interpretability, and evaluation of complex model representations. Methodologies like GEOSTEER leverage geodesic optimization for norm-preserving activation steering in large language models to modify behavior at inference time without causing representation collapse. Simultaneously, theoretical investigations highlight critical pitfalls in interpretability tools, demonstrating that truth probes fitted in compliant contexts suffer from perfect aliasing where true reporting and task compliance cannot be distinguished. To better evaluate real-world model reliability, counterfactual marginalisation frameworks measure classifier invariance against demographic or domain-specific nuisance variables at test time.

Fundamental algorithmic developments across forecasting, ordinal classification, and statistical calibration address long-standing loss function limitations. In time series forecasting, the Halo method shows that estimating heteroscedastic scale parameters directly improves point-estimate precision rather than serving solely as a tool for uncertainty quantification. In ordinal classification, adaptive margin losses mitigate "center-class hedging"—a common failure mode where neural networks output middle-ground classes to minimize expected loss. Additionally, conformal prediction frameworks are expanding to transfer calibration coverage guarantees from a labeled source domain to an unlabelled target domain where data exchangeability assumptions break down.

Domain-specific applications illustrate how deep learning models are tailored to physical systems, maritime trajectory modeling, and distributed networks. Integrations of frozen text-to-image diffusion models into classical topology optimization allow engineers to generate bio-inspired structural designs without fine-tuning specialized networks. For spatio-temporal dynamics and decision-making, multimodal transformer architectures combine Mixture-of-Experts with language model priors for long-term vessel trajectory prediction, while goal-conditioned reinforcement learning formulations construct dense reward functions directly from directed state graph topologies.

- [M3-Former: Multimodal Transformer with Mixture-of-Experts for Long-Term Vessel Trajectory Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10559): Combines static vessel attributes and navigational intent using large language model priors and a Mixture-of-Experts transformer to improve long-term vessel trajectory forecasting.
- [Halo: Improving forecast accuracy through heteroscedastic estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10589): Demonstrates that estimating scale parameters alongside location parameters via heteroscedastic estimation improves point forecast accuracy in time series models.
- [Zero-shot rib design: merging training-free generative prior with topology optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10643): Integrates frozen text-to-image diffusion models with topology optimization to synthesize bio-inspired structural designs without specialized retraining.
- [Byzantine-Robust Federated Fire Detection with a Rotating Coordinator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10647): Introduces a federated learning framework with rotating coordinators for privacy-preserving indoor fire detection resilient to Byzantine clients and bandwidth limits.
- [Artificial Intelligence Algorithms for the Detection of Pathologies Related to Lung Cancer through Image Analysis using Convolutional Neural Networks and Data Augmentation: a systematic mapping of the literature](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10652): Provides a systematic literature mapping of deep learning and data augmentation methods applied to lung cancer pathology detection in medical imaging.
- [GEOSTEER: Geodesic Optimization for Activation Steering in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10658): Applies geodesic optimization to maintain norm-preserving hidden activations during inference-time activation steering in large language models.
- [Conformal Calibration Transfer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10737): Extends conformal prediction guarantees to target spaces where labeled calibration data is only available in a source domain.
- [The Truth Was Never Gone: Perfect Aliasing in Compliant-Context Truth Probes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10739): Identifies perfect aliasing as a structural limitation in truth probes, showing that probes trained where truth and task compliance coincide cannot distinguish between the two targets.
- [Adaptive Margin Ordinal Loss: Penalizing Center-Class Hedging in Ordinal Classification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10752): Proposes an adaptive margin loss to prevent neural networks in ordinal classification tasks from conservatively predicting center classes.
- [A Bellman Optimality Equation for Plasticity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10776): Formulates a theoretical framework based on Bellman optimality equations to analyze plasticity and empowerment tradeoffs in continual reinforcement learning.
- [Counterfactual Marginalisation: Framework for Evaluating Robustness to Nuisance Variables](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10778): Proposes a test-time evaluation framework using counterfactual generators to measure classifier robustness against nuisance variables.
- [From Connectivity to Rewards: Dense Reward Learning with Directed State Graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10781): Utilizes directed state graph topologies to construct dense reward functions in goal-conditioned hierarchical reinforcement learning.
