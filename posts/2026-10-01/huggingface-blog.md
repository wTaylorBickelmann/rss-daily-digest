The Allen Institute for AI has introduced Olmo-core 3, a dedicated open-source framework designed to support the scalable training of large Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models. As non-dense architectures become increasingly central to state-of-the-art machine learning research, standardized infrastructure is necessary to streamline training workflows across distributed systems.

Training large MoE architectures presents distinct computational and routing challenges compared to standard dense transformers. Olmo-core 3 addresses these requirements by providing open, scalable training infrastructure built specifically to manage the resource demands of MoE workloads.

By releasing this core tooling to the public, the project helps broaden access to large-scale model training capabilities. This enables developers and researchers to analyze, reproduce, and build custom MoE systems without relying on proprietary training stacks.
