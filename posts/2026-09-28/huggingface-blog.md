Hcompany has introduced Holo4, a new effort focused on powering generalist computer-use agents. As artificial intelligence systems expand from foundational language and vision tasks toward active digital manipulation, models capable of interacting directly with software interfaces are becoming a central area of development.

Generalist computer-use agents aim to perform complex, multi-step actions across computer environments, translating user intent into interface actions. The introduction of Holo4 addresses the ongoing technical effort to create models that can reliably navigate software applications and execute operational workflows.
