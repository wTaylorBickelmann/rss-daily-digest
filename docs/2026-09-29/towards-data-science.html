In parallel, there is an increasing emphasis on refined model choices and evaluation rigor. Beyond accelerating baseline coding productivity, AI integration is expanding the scope of the data scientist role, fundamentally altering responsibilities related to project ownership, technical judgment, and long-term career trajectory.
