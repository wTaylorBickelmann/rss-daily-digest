Standardizing Multilingual Text-to-Speech Evaluation with the Open TTS Leaderboard

Wednesday, 30 September 2026 · That day’s posts

Hugging Face has introduced the Open TTS Leaderboard to provide standardized, scalable evaluation for text-to-speech (TTS) and voice cloning systems. As speech generation models rapidly expand across diverse languages and architectures, established evaluation mechanisms are required to consistently compare audio quality, naturalness, and voice cloning accuracy.

The leaderboard establishes a unified benchmarking framework to assess speech synthesis models at scale. By evaluating systems on standardized multilingual datasets and metrics, this initiative enables researchers and developers to transparently compare model capabilities and track progress in synthetic voice generation.
