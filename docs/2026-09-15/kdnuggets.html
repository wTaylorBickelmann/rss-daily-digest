Streamlining AI Development: No-Code Automation and Free Educational Curricula

Tuesday, 15 September 2026 · That day’s posts

Google Labs is expanding its tooling for rapid prototyping with Google Opal, an application designed to build AI mini-applications directly from natural language prompts. Operating on top of an internal framework named Breadboard, Opal allows practitioners to convert text instructions into functional automation workflows without writing manual code.

At the same time, open educational infrastructure continues to support foundational technical learning. Microsoft has made available five free GitHub-hosted courses covering a broad range of topics, including core data science, traditional machine learning, generative AI, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), fine-tuning, and autonomous AI agents.

These developments illustrate two distinct entry points for modern AI adoption: simplifying workflow construction through prompt-driven no-code platforms, and offering structured, open-access curricula for developers seeking deeper technical knowledge in advanced AI architectures.
